TallyTick Privacy Policy

Information about data handling in TallyTick for iPhone.

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · App version 1.0

About this policy

This policy covers the TallyTick iPhone app provided by FJ Develop. For privacy questions, contact us through TallyTick Support.

Calculations and local storage

TallyTick performs time conversions and operation calculations on your device. It does not require an account or sign-in.

The app saves your operation list and language preference locally using iOS app storage. A saved entry contains an identifier generated by the app, an optional name, a duration and a repetition count. The identifier identifies an entry, not your device or an account.

The converter’s current hours, minutes and seconds are held temporarily while the app is running. They are not saved as part of the operation list unless you separately create an entry.

Data sent from the app

TallyTick does not send your operation entries or calculations to FJ Develop. Version 1.0 contains no app-operated network service, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or third-party crash reporting SDK. The app does not implement iCloud synchronization.

Copying results

When you tap Copy result or Copy total, TallyTick writes the displayed result to the system clipboard. It does not read clipboard contents. A copied result can be pasted into other apps and may be available through Apple’s Universal Clipboard, depending on your device settings. Clipboard handling outside TallyTick is controlled by the operating system and the apps where you paste the result.

Deleting data and device backups

You can delete individual entries or clear the operation list in the app. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data from the device. Copies you have pasted elsewhere are separate.

iOS or computer backups may include local app data, depending on your settings. Clearing the list or deleting the app does not erase an existing backup. Backup storage, restoration and deletion are managed through Apple’s services or your backup software.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store, device backup and diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. This is separate from TallyTick’s own local calculations and storage.

Support requests

If you choose to email support, FJ Develop receives your email address, message and any attachments you send. Your email service and our Outlook email service also process the correspondence. This is separate from using the app; TallyTick does not automatically send a support message or attach your operation list.

You can contact TallyTick Support with questions about personal information you have sent by email, including requests for access or deletion.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if TallyTick’s data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
