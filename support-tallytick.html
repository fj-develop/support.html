TallyTick Support

Help with time conversions, operation times and repetitions.

Contact support

Email fj.develop@outlook.com for help or feedback.

For a calculation issue, include the durations and repetitions you entered, the result you expected and the result shown. Please also include your iPhone model, iOS version and TallyTick version from Settings.

Only include information needed to explain the issue. Remove personal or confidential details from screenshots before sending them.

How do I convert time?

Open the Converter tab and select hours, minutes and seconds. One hour and three minutes gives 63 minutes. Seconds that do not make up a full minute are shown separately. Tap Copy result to use the result elsewhere.

How do I calculate repeated operations?

Open the Time calculator tab, tap Add time and save a duration with an optional name. Tap the saved entry to change its repetitions. Each entry contributes its duration multiplied by its repetition count to the total.

Repetitions must be a whole number from 1 to 10,000. For example, 2 minutes 30 seconds repeated four times gives 10 minutes.

How do I change language?

Open Settings using the gear button, then choose English, Dansk, Deutsch or System language. English is the fallback for unsupported languages.

Where are my times saved?

The operation list is saved locally on your iPhone. The app does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization. Converter selections are temporary and are not saved as operation entries.

How do I delete times?

Swipe an entry to reveal Delete, or open it and use Delete. To remove the entire list, open the list actions menu, choose Clear all times and confirm. This removes entries from the app; it does not erase copies you pasted elsewhere or existing device backups.

Why is a time rounded?

Displayed durations are rounded to the nearest second.
