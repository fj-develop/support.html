Convert time

Select hours, minutes and seconds to see total minutes and any remaining seconds. For example, 1 hour and 3 minutes becomes 63 minutes.

Combine operations

Add named durations and see their combined total. Select a saved operation to edit its time or set between 1 and 10,000 repetitions.

Copy the result

Copy a conversion or total to the clipboard with a tap. Use English, Danish or German, with support for Light and Dark Mode.

From individual steps to a total

2 min 30 sec × 4 + 5 min = 15 min

For example, add a 2-minute-30-second operation, set it to four repetitions, then add a five-minute operation. TallyTick combines them into one total.

Saved on your iPhone

Your operation list and language preference are saved locally. No account is required. Read the Privacy Policy for details about storage, copying and device backups.
