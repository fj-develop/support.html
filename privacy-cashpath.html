Privacy Policy

CashPath Planner — Privacy and transparency for your financial information.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

CashPath Planner may store the following information that you enter directly in the app:

  • Account details, such as account names and balances
  • Budget categories and custom categories
  • Transaction history
  • Financial goals and forecasts

All data is stored locally on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to provide app functionality, such as budgeting, tracking, forecasting, and helping you manage your financial overview.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your information is stored locally on your Mac. FJ Develop does not operate a server that stores your financial data.

5. Your Rights

You remain in control of your data. You may delete information inside the app or remove the app and its local data from your device.

Disclaimer

CashPath Planner is a personal planning tool. While the app is designed to provide helpful calculations and projections, results should be treated as estimates only. FJ Develop is not responsible for financial decisions made based on the app's data or projections.
