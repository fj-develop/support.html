Privacy Policy

RicePerfect — Simple cooking tools with privacy built in.

Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. Information RicePerfect Stores

RicePerfect may store the following information that you create or select in the app:

  • Favorite rice recipes, including rice type, amounts, cooking time, and resting time
  • Active and completed cooking timers
  • App preferences, such as language, measurement units, appearance, and timer sound

All of this information is stored locally on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

The information is used only to provide app functionality, including calculations, saved favorites, cooking timers, and your chosen settings.

3. Notifications

RicePerfect may request permission to send local notifications when a cooking or resting timer finishes. These notifications are generated on your device. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Recipe Sharing

RicePerfect lets you share a recipe as a file, image, or text. Sharing happens only when you choose to use the share function. The selected app or service will handle the shared content according to its own privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing and Tracking

FJ Develop does not collect, sell, rent, or share your RicePerfect data. RicePerfect does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

6. Data Storage

FJ Develop does not operate a server that stores your RicePerfect data. Data created in the app remains on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete favorite recipes and timers inside the app. Deleting RicePerfect from your device removes data stored by the app. Copies that you previously shared or exported are not controlled by RicePerfect and must be deleted separately.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if RicePerfect's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this website.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or RicePerfect, please visit the Support page.
