Privacy Policy

PawJournal — Privacy and transparency for your pet's information.

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The app may store the following information:

  • Weight data
  • Pet health information
  • Reminders and notes

All data is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

The data is used only to provide app functionality, such as tracking, managing records, and reminders.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, your stored data is also deleted.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your data at any time by deleting the app
  • Contact us if you have questions about your data
