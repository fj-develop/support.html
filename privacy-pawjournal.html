Privacy Policy

PawJournal — Privacy and transparency for your pet's information.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The app may store the following information:

  • Weight data
  • Pet health information
  • Reminders and notes

All data is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

The data is used only to provide app functionality, such as tracking, managing records, and reminders.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

If you choose to export your pet's information, PawJournal creates an export file containing only the data you choose to include. You decide how and where that file is shared using the options available on your device.

PawJournal does not automatically transmit exported data to FJ Develop or any third party.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device.

Export files are created only when you request them. If you choose to include attached PDF documents during export, those PDF files are included in the export file. If you do not select them, they are not included.

Imported pet data is added to PawJournal only when you choose to import a compatible export file.

If you delete the app, your locally stored app data is also deleted.

5. Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your data at any time.
  • Export or import your pet's information whenever you choose.
  • Choose whether attached PDF documents are included in an export.
